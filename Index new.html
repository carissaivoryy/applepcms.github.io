Company History

Four eras of Apple’s development

Apple’s story can be understood through product vision, strategic mistakes, leadership return, and the rise of its global ecosystem.

1976–1984

The Garage & The Mac

Jobs and Wozniak start Apple in a suburban garage. Apple II helps kickstart the personal computer boom, and the Macintosh makes the mouse and visual desktop interface mainstream.

1985–1996

The Downward Spiral

Jobs is forced out. Without his product vision, Apple bloats its lineup, loses ground to Microsoft Windows, and moves toward bankruptcy.

1997–2010

The Turnaround & Mobile Boom

Jobs returns as CEO, cuts underperforming projects, and refocuses Apple on clean design. Apple launches iMac, iPod, and the iPhone.

2011–Present

The Multi-Trillion Dollar Ecosystem

Under Tim Cook, Apple becomes a global powerhouse, expanding into wearables and profitable services like iCloud and Apple Music.
